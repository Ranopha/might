## Description

Might is a private companion that helps people recognize where their experience,
interests or resources may be useful. It begins as a quiet orb, takes an original
OpenAI-generated form, and gets to know someone through natural conversation.
Source-linked living memories stay editable and private in Convex.

For this hackathon slice, Firecrawl reads one real public volunteer source.
OpenAI interprets the situation, explains a possible overlap and asks at most one
clarifying question. When the person chooses to explore it, Might prepares a
private introduction. The recipient, complete message, private fields and payload
fingerprint are reviewed before explicit approval and a separate Send action.
AgentMail handles the outbound message and verified inbound events; Convex updates
the connection live. A reply excerpt receives a source-bound OpenAI summary.

The demonstration uses a fictional persona and project-controlled email inboxes.
It does not claim a partnership with the nonprofit whose public page is cited.
Connected means two-way contact, without a deal, payment or scheduling commitment.
